Light Path – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Light Path is a puzzle game for Android. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: Light Path does not collect, send, share or sell any personal information.

What the app collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no tracking of any kind. The app does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

What is stored on your phone

To remember your game, the app saves a small amount of data only on your device:

  • your progress (stars earned and levels unlocked), your daily-puzzle streak and the number of hints you hold;
  • levels you started but have not finished;
  • your settings (sound, vibration and light/dark appearance).

This data never leaves your phone. You can erase it at any time with Settings → Reset progress, by clearing the app's data in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The app can make your phone vibrate (so taps feel responsive) and keep it awake for the moment a sound effect plays. Android also lists the normal "network access" permissions for apps built with the framework used here, but Light Path does not connect to the internet or send any data anywhere.

Third parties

The app includes no advertising or analytics code from third parties. It is built with the open-source Expo and React Native frameworks, which run entirely on your device. If you install the app from Google Play, Google handles the download under Google's own privacy policy; the app itself receives nothing from that.

Children

Because the app collects no information at all, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
